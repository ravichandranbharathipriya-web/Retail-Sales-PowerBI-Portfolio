Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence project designed to explore retail sales performance, customer behaviour, product trends, and regional sales insights using Microsoft Power BI.

View GitHub Project

Project Overview

This project transforms retail data into visual insights to help understand sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and business opportunities.

The Power BI report includes sales analysis, customer analytics, regional performance, monthly and seasonal sales, and customer behaviour.

Tools and Technologies

Power BI DAX Power Query Microsoft Excel Data Visualization Business Intelligence

Key Project Objectives

  • Analyze sales and order performance.
  • Explore customer purchasing behaviour.
  • Compare regional and product performance.
  • Identify monthly and seasonal sales patterns.
  • Present business insights through dashboards.
